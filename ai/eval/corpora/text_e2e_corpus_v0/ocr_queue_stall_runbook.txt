OCR queue stall runbook.

Step 1: confirm Redis is reachable and inspect the pending queue length.
Step 2: check whether any worker has an active claim token for the stalled job.
Step 3: if the claim is stale, move the job back to the pending queue with the original correlation id.
Step 4: restart the worker only after the database status and Redis queue agree.
Escalate to the platform owner if two recovery attempts leave the same job stalled.
